# Calendar View

Calendar View is a trip-planning overview, not a calendar replacement.

Supported modes:

- Trip overview: one tile per itinerary day.
- Week overview: first seven visible calendar cells from the trip month.
- Month overview: 42-cell month grid around the trip start month.

Calendar tiles highlight travel days, busy days, free days, warnings, weather summaries when loaded, accommodation check-in/check-out anchors, item counts, and estimated daily item cost.

Public share pages can render Agenda, Timeline, and Calendar in read-only mode. They do not expose collaboration, activity history, comments, edit controls, or internal conflict metadata beyond simple viewer-safe warning labels.
